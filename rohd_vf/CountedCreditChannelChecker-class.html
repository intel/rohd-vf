CountedCreditChannelChecker

CountedCreditChannelChecker class

Checks counted debit and return events for independent or shared pools.

Multiple pools may be debited and returned in the same cycle. For an atomic multi-resource transaction, connect the gate's accepted debit counts rather than its unaccepted requirements.
